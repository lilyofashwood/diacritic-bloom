Bring every bloom home

Printable ASCII plus tab and line breaks round-trips exactly through this codec. Other clusters pass through, but original styled symbols can become ambiguous. A validated recipe saves both source and encoded text explicitly.

NFC/NFD can reorder or decompose marks. NFKC/NFKD can also erase the styled alphabet. Do not normalize saved specimens when exact code points matter. The decoder preserves ordinary accents; recipes retain the original text and its exact decoration.

Preservation and rendering notes

The literal original contains U+0344, which canonically decomposes into U+0308 + U+0301, plus U+0340, which decomposes to U+0300. Its U+035C and U+035D marks can visually span neighboring letters. They are preserved in the specimen but excluded from random generation. The code points stay exact; each screen's fonts and line spacing shape the visible bloom.
