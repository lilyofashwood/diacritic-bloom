These are Unicode characters, not a downloadable font. The device's fonts and layout engine decide how the marks actually sit.

What “reversible” means here

Printable ASCII plus tab and line breaks round-trips exactly through this codec. Other clusters pass through, but original styled symbols can become ambiguous. A validated recipe saves both source and encoded text explicitly.

NFC/NFD can reorder or decompose marks. NFKC/NFKD can also erase the styled alphabet. Do not normalize saved specimens when exact code points matter. The decoder is not a general-purpose accent remover and this is not encryption.

Preservation and rendering notes

The literal original contains U+0344, which canonically decomposes into U+0308 + U+0301, plus U+0340, which decomposes to U+0300. Its U+035C and U+035D marks can visually span neighboring letters. They are preserved in the specimen but excluded from random generation. Screens and apps can display the same sequence differently or clip tall stacks. No pixel-identical rendering is promised.
